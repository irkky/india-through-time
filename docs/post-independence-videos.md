# Post-Independence to 2025 — video guide

Researched and checked on **4 November 2026**. **57 distinct selections** cover all **five periods
and 55 existing timeline records**: **56 YouTube players** and **one publisher-only ICC card**.
The timeline still ends in **2025**. Publication dates of retrospective programmes do not create
new historical events or change existing dates.

## Research and selection

Searched original publisher uploads for every topic, comparing university lectures, public
broadcasters, institutional archives, original news reporting, documentaries and educational
explainers. This is a curated collection, not a claim to have found every available recording
or watched every selected programme in full. Selection considered specific relevance, source
identity, duration, scope, accessibility and metadata consistency.

The original watch response for all 56 selected YouTube recordings returned `status=OK` and
`playableInEmbed=true`, and the oEmbed title matched. These checks support offering an embed;
they do not guarantee playback for every country, account or future date. The ICC highlights
candidate returned HTTP 401 during oEmbed verification, so its original publisher page is
provided as a source-only card. No media files are downloaded or rehosted.

## Videos in timeline order

Each card carries the publisher, duration where verified, source link, description and
historical-scope note. The manifest records exact event/period placement and the metadata
verification. Some short archive clips supplement longer lectures. Shared videos appear once
per reader period, even where they accompany two related dated records.

## Historical scope and editorial decisions

- **Constitution:** the shared programme accompanies adoption in November 1949 and commencement in January 1950. Independence remains dated to 1947; republic status is not a second independence date.
- **Hyderabad, wars and Kargil:** military narratives and maps do not establish the entire civilian experience or settle territorial claims. Cards identify institutional or journalistic perspectives and retain the record’s other sources.
- **Pather Panchali:** BFI supplies an authorised critical introduction, not a free full film. The film is from 1955, Cannes recognition from 1956. The [Cannes record](https://www.festival-cannes.com/en/f/pather-panchali-2/) is linked separately.
- **Development:** the CEC Green Revolution lecture explicitly addresses uneven change. The [NDDB film](https://www.nddb.coop/about/film) represents the institution’s own account; it is not evidence that every producer experienced identical benefits. Planned targets, implementation and outcomes remain distinct.
- **Chipko:** the Bahuguna-focused programme is contextualised with the wider movement, women organisers and local livelihood concerns. It is not labelled a complete history of every campaign.
- **Emergency and 1977:** pair retrospective teaching with the government’s original announcement and later Janata speeches. Primary political speech is evidence of what was said, not automatic confirmation that the speaker’s interpretation is correct.
- **1983 cricket:** the [ICC viewing page](https://www.icc-cricket.com/tournaments/cricketworldcup/news/on-this-day-india-win-the-1983-world-cup) remains the authorised fallback; no unverified third-party full-match upload is substituted.
- **1984 anti-Sikh violence, Bhopal, Babri Masjid, Gujarat and Mumbai:** survivor testimony, contemporaneous coverage, investigative journalism and legal findings are different kinds of evidence. Cards describe their limits and indicate potentially distressing content. No autoplay exposes visitors to footage unexpectedly.
- **Mizoram:** regional broadcaster coverage is retained; English captions are not guaranteed. Accord and statehood remain separately dated.
- **Courts and rights:** short explainers are orientation, not replacements for judgments. The 2017 privacy ruling is distinct from later Aadhaar cases; the 2018 Navtej ruling did not itself establish marriage equality. The selected Navtej news report replaces a candidate whose description confused Section 377 with a constitutional article.
- **Jammu and Kashmir:** the selected parliamentary recording shows voting and passage. It does not claim to explain all effects of Article 370 measures, territorial disputes, civilian experience or subsequent litigation.
- **Public health:** UNICEF’s polio history distinguishes the last wild-polio case from certification. The 2020 lockdown report and 2021 vaccination rollout are historical reporting, not current medical guidance or national coverage estimates.
- **Currency and tax:** the 2016 announcement is paired with a later analysis; the GST programme concerns changes in its first year. Historical rates, transaction limits and exchange procedures are not current instructions.
- **Farm laws:** the selected analysis concerns the announcement to withdraw the laws, not passage of the repeal Act. Existing dates remain unchanged.
- **G20:** the film is linked from the [UN in India’s 2023 account](https://india.un.org/en/245545-g20-leaders-adopt-declaration-new-delhi). The YouTube channel currently identifies as G20 USA; this name is preserved transparently rather than attributing the upload to the UN. Summit ceremony does not prove implementation of its commitments.
- **2024 election:** ECI’s short film focuses on administrative facilities and accessibility. Final results and turnout still come from the record’s ECI tables, not this montage.
- **Space missions:** ISRO’s long landing telecast and [SpaDeX presentation](https://www.isro.gov.in/spadex_docking.html) distinguish camera footage, commentary and visualisation. The January 2025 docking is separate from subsequent undocking and power-transfer tests. Proposed applications do not extend the timeline beyond 2025.
- **Maratha forts:** a 2025 heritage inscription is not an early modern military event. All twelve components must be distinguished from a photograph of a single fort.

### Further material worth adding

Longer dedicated explanations would improve the brief First Plan, bank-nationalisation, UPI,
Navtej, tsunami-interview, 2024 election and heritage-introduction cards. Useful additions include
translated local testimony, complete regional accounts of integration, a documented results
analysis for the 2024 election and specialist discussion of the effects of the 2019 reorganisation.
These are coverage limits, not invented missing recordings. Existing tagged image, map, oral-history
and statistical-series requests remain intact. New footage should retain publisher permission,
precise dates, language/caption information and clearly identified historical scope.

### Excluded or replaced results

Rejected unauthorised-looking full-film and documentary reuploads in favour of original publishers.
Replaced very short Emergency and polio clips with longer explanatory material. National Geographic’s
Mangalyaan teaser was replaced with a longer mission explainer. Used the full Pokhran programme
instead of promotional teasers. The ICC YouTube candidate could not be verified for embedding;
this does not prove that it is unavailable in every region. A recent basic-structure programme was
not needed after selecting an established case explanation.

## Playback and maintenance

Players use the existing click-to-load, privacy-enhanced YouTube widget. No autoplay or eager
thumbnail loading is added. Loading another video stops the previous player; closing the evidence
dialog unloads it. Every embedded card retains **Watch on YouTube**, and the ICC card offers
**Open video source**. These links work without JavaScript. No automatic redirect interrupts reading.
Publisher restrictions may change after verification.

The selection includes English and Hindi programmes and regional Mizoram coverage. Some publisher
titles use Hindi script. Caption availability is controlled by publishers; no universal English
subtitle coverage is claimed. The seven earlier external film references remain available.

- `src/data/timeline/post-independence/videos.ts`: catalogue and explicit mappings.
- `docs/post-independence-video-manifest.json`: source metadata and embedding-check evidence.
- `tests/post-independence-videos.test.cjs`: coverage, source-only fallback, chronology cutoff and scope checks.

Run `npm run check`, `npm run build`, start the production server, then:

```powershell
$env:VIDEOS_CHAPTER = 'republic'
node scripts/browser-videos.cjs http://localhost:3135
```

The optional browser check exercises all 57 reader cards at four widths, deferred loading,
one active player, source links and dialog cleanup. Its live sample is the ECI first-election
film; it does not watch every recording in full.
