# Prehistoric India: visual evidence inventory

Reviewed 27 October 2026. The chapter retains 24 chronological entries. Twelve new local media files serve eleven entries; the two existing Palaeolithic collections remain in place. Thirteen entries now have galleries. Every entry has a specific image request in its evidence panel.

## How to explore

Scroll through Locate, Examine and Interpret, or select a stage. Open **Explore images & full evidence** to select images, read the complete caption, follow the publication and licence links, and open the full image. Plates retain their scales and labels. Images load locally; no third-party video loads automatically.

## Sources, rights and chronology

The [media manifest](prehistory-media-manifest.json) records each local file, original download URL, source, creator credit, licence, SHA-256 digest and event placement. Captions distinguish multi-period plates from a single dated assemblage. Modern photographs and research publication dates are not prehistoric event dates. The overall journey still ends in 2025. The 2026 Brahmagiri paper investigates a prehistoric feature; it does not add a 2026 timeline entry.

- Mehrgarh figures are CC BY-NC-ND 4.0: supplied unchanged for noncommercial use. Keep complete figures and attribution; adaptations or commercial reuse require separate permission.
- Brahmagiri is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: retain attribution and the noncommercial/share-alike conditions.
- Bhimbetka is CC BY-SA 4.0; its capture date is 2022, not the date of the painting.
- Singi Talav, Dhaba and the Bates regional map are CC BY 4.0. PLOS figures retain the Creative Commons Attribution statement from their individual articles; no unverified licence version has been invented.
- Previously supplied Lower/Middle Palaeolithic images still need their original provenance and reuse terms. They are not silently relicensed by this update.

## Candidates deliberately not substituted

Harappa/HARP Ravi and Kot Diji phase images need permission; their published catalogues are useful sourcing leads. Dhaba satellite imagery carries separate third-party credits. A Commons Navdatoli goblet dated 1300 BCE does not match this entry's 1700-1500 BCE range. Generic handaxes, unrelated Bagor search results and images of neighbouring sites were excluded. The remaining requests identify the actual site and phase needed rather than filling gaps with lookalikes.

## Adding evidence later

Add the permitted file beneath `public/assets/images/prehistoric_india/`, register complete metadata in `src/data/scenes/prehistory-galleries.ts`, and update the matching request in `src/data/timeline/prehistory/evidence.ts`. Preserve original scale bars and figure labels. Record specimen/site/phase, photographer or publication, source URL, licence and any date uncertainty. Update the manifest and run `npm run check`, `npm run build` and the browser checks. Do not remove a botanical-specimen request merely because a regional map is available.
