The 12 entries without suitable images retain specific acquisition requests; all 29 entries retain an additional evidence tag.

See the [authoring guide](independence-movement-content.md), [new media manifest](independence-researched-media-manifest.json) and [original photo manifest](independence-media-manifest.json). Download URLs, credits, licences, capture dates and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the new manifest.

## How to complete a tag

Supply the exact object or site requested, its catalogue or archive identifier, historical date, photograph/recording date, creator, permission or reuse licence, source URL and explanatory caption. A later painting must retain its actual production date. Keep research and map requests until their evidence is integrated; adding a contextual site photograph does not fulfil a request for a manuscript.

- Region: Indian diaspora and Punjab
- Date note: Calendar date or conventional political span. Read the Urdu and Gurmukhi editions as political interventions, not neutral reports. Diaspora activism did not speak for every migrant.
