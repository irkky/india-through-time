# Colonial Era & British Rule: video guide

Reviewed **4 November 2026**. Thirty-four selected publisher videos accompany all five
periods and thirty-six records. One lecture is reused across two periods, producing
thirty-five reading-section cards. All selected uploads currently permit embedding.

## Selected viewing

heritage films provide visual companions. This is a curated search, not an exhaustive
index of all available recordings or a claim that every full lecture was watched and
independently fact-checked. Broader background is labelled on the cards.

## Historical fit

- **Company power and revenue:** eighteenth-century background, separate lectures on the Regulating and Pitt's Acts, Permanent Settlement, Mysore, Marathas and resistance. The 1770 famine receives period context, not the recording on the 1943 catastrophe.
- **Conquest, education and reform:** social reform, colonial education, the Phules, Punjab, railways, Adivasi societies and Awadh. The Phule episode is explicitly labelled a scripted historical dramatisation; actors and dialogue are not contemporary evidence.
- **Rebellion and Crown rule:** a longer 1857 lecture, the 1858 Act, agrarian relations, Bourne's photography and the terminus film. The later monumental station is not the first passenger service of 1853.
- **Public politics:** Congress, Birsa, Swadeshi and Indian First World War experiences. Birsa's upload description incorrectly places the movement in the early nineteenth century; the card flags the error and retains the chapter's late-nineteenth-century chronology. The programme is a modern commemorative account.
- **Mass protest and transfer:** Rowlatt, Jallianwala Bagh, the 1919 and 1935 Acts, Non-Cooperation, Civil Disobedience, Quit India, the 1943 famine, negotiations, the 1947 Act and migration. These are distinct stages, not one sequence compressed into a single date.

The First World War discussion focuses on Indian combatants, labourers and civilians.
The famine session was selected for its Bengal subject within a comparative conference;
no unrelated foreign-history chapter was added. The organiser's
[conference account](https://holodomor.ca/communism-and-hunger-the-ukrainian-chinese-kazakh-and-soviet-famines-in-comparative-perspective/)
identifies Janam Mukherjee and the talk. The [UNESCO site record](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/945/)
accompanies the terminus film. Publication dates and historical dates remain separate.

## Research decisions and remaining gaps

Original public watch metadata and oEmbed titles were checked for every selected upload.
The [manifest](colonial-video-manifest.json) records publisher, duration, publication time,
verification date, placement and embedding evidence. A Rowlatt upload with a music-course
description was replaced by Ramachandra Guha's original Bangalore International Centre
talk. Anonymous reposts, unrelated current-affairs results and brief ceremony clips were
not used to fill missing historical coverage. Caption and audio-language availability
depend on each publisher and are not promised across the collection.

## Playback and maintenance

Select **Load video player**, then Play. The shared privacy-enhanced player loads on
request, without autoplay or eager third-party thumbnails. Loading another player stops
the previous one; closing the player or evidence dialog unloads it. Every card retains
**Watch on YouTube** for blocked or unavailable playback, including without JavaScript.
No automatic redirect interrupts reading. No videos are downloaded or rehosted, and
image licences are not assumed to cover recordings. Embedding permissions can change.

- `src/data/timeline/colonial/videos.ts`: catalogue and explicit period/record mappings.
- `colonial/events.ts` and `colonial-india.ts`: attach selections to the existing sections.
- `tests/colonial-videos.test.cjs`: coverage, manifest consistency, exact placement and key scope qualifications.

Run `npm run check`, `npm run build`, start the production server and run:

```powershell
$env:VIDEOS_CHAPTER = 'colonial'
node scripts/browser-videos.cjs http://localhost:3132
```

The optional browser test checks all thirty-five card instances at four widths, deferred
loading, source links, a single player, dialog cleanup and no-JavaScript access. Its live
sample is CEC's 1857 lecture; it does not test all recordings in full.
