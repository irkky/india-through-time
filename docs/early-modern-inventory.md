Reviewed **29 October 2026**. **54 entries**, **220 evidence sections**, **33 illustrated galleries**, **33 distinct local images** (28 newly researched and five existing Met images), and **four external UNESCO film guides**. The 21 entries without suitable images retain specific acquisition requests; all 54 entries retain an additional evidence tag.

See the [authoring guide](early-modern-india-content.md), [new media manifest](early-modern-researched-media-manifest.json) and [original Met manifest](early-modern-media-manifest.json). Download URLs, credits, licences, capture dates and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in the new manifest.

## How to complete a tag

Supply the exact object or site requested, its catalogue or archive identifier, historical date, photograph/recording date, creator, permission or reuse licence, source URL and explanatory caption. A later painting must retain its actual production date. Keep research and map requests until their evidence is integrated; adding a contextual site photograph does not fulfil a request for a manuscript.
