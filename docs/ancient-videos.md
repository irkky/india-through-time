## Why these selections

The selection prioritises substantive explanations from CEC, Vidya-mitra's educational
courseware, the University of Hyderabad and Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute.
UNESCO's chanting film and the Department of Science & Technology's Dholavira film
provide short, clearly identified heritage companions. The focused Painted Grey Ware
video is an educator's interpretation with a published reading list, not an excavation
report or an institutional finding.

This is a curated selection, not an exhaustive index of every available video. Publisher
metadata, descriptions, institutional catalogue references, duration and embedding flags
were checked; the full content of every lecture has not been watched or independently
fact-checked. Language and subtitles depend on the publisher's player. The source
[manifest](ancient-video-manifest.json) records exact titles, URLs, publication dates,
period/event mappings and the embedding checks.

- **Cities of the Indus:** a substantial CEC introduction accompanies the urban records.
  Dholavira additionally receives its own short film. Neither film dates every structure
  or substitutes for the newer Rakhigarhi excavation evidence.
- **Regional transformations:** an introductory decline lecture is paired with Ratnagar's
  2013 discussion of social and political explanations. Her argument is a scholarly
  perspective, not a settled conclusion or a review of later climate studies. Early
  Ganga?Vindhya ironworking instead receives the metals lecture; it is not presented as
  a continuation of the Harappan-decline debate.
- **Vedic oral worlds:** CEC introduces the period. The Rigveda record receives Bhandarkar's
  longer textual lecture and UNESCO's modern chanting film; the later Vedic record uses
  the broad history lecture. The Upanishads record receives the full Chandogya episode,
  rather than the series teaser. These are different kinds of evidence, not interchangeable
  recordings of ancient life. The publisher's Rigveda chapter markers are retained in its
  viewing description; no invented timestamps are supplied.
- **Iron Age regional worlds:** the course lecture supplies wider social/economic context;
  the PGW companion discusses a specific pottery debate. The question in its title is not
  evidence identifying a language, ethnicity or migration. Its 2026 publication date does
  not add a 2026 historical event to the timeline.

General city lectures accompany the craft, seal, weight, farming and exchange records
as background; they are not labelled as dedicated films of every specimen or site.
Harappa and Mohenjo-daro belong to this Indian-subcontinental history even though the
sites are in present-day Pakistan. Modern recognition dates, recording dates, ancient
composition and excavation dates remain separate.

## Candidates not used

A college-linked iron-technology video returned HTTP 404 and was excluded. A Bhandarkar
Upanishads teaser was replaced with the full Chandogya lecture. The Harappa-hosted Ratnagar
page could not be retrieved from this environment; the original University of Hyderabad
upload was found and verified instead. Generic exam shorts and anonymous reuploads were
not preferred over the original teaching sources. The existing IGNCA recitation link
remains in the evidence references, but was not promoted to a new player because a stable
individual embed could not be verified.

## Playback and maintenance

Open **Explore videos, images & evidence** on an ancient scene, or use its reading section.
Select **Load video player**, then press Play inside the publisher's player. No third-party
player or thumbnail is fetched before selection. Loading a different card unloads the
previous player; closing the player or evidence dialog stops it. Direct **Watch on YouTube**
links remain visible and usable without JavaScript. No automatic redirect interrupts reading.
No video files are downloaded, redistributed or added to the site's hosting storage.
Embedding permission can change; the external links provide the fallback.

Edit `src/data/timeline/ancient/videos.ts` for the catalogue, period guides and narrower
record-specific selections. `ancient/events.ts` attaches the record selection;
`ancient-india.ts` attaches each reading-section guide. The existing shared
`HistoricalVideos` component handles playback, sizing and source links.

Run `npm run check` and `npm run build`. Start the production server, then run the
focused browser check in PowerShell with the actual server URL:

```powershell
$env:VIDEOS_CHAPTER = 'ancient'
node scripts/browser-videos.cjs http://localhost:3128
```

This optional test checks every card at 1440, 768, 390 and 320px, blocked-provider
fallback links, deferred loading, one loaded player, dialog closure and no-JavaScript
source links. It then clicks the CEC Harappan player's Play button and saves screenshots
for inspection; this is a sample playback check, not complete playback verification of
all eleven videos. Set `VIDEOS_CHAPTER` to `prehistory` to run the earlier chapter checks.
